Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

The short version. Kova keeps the money details you give it so it can explain your money back to you. We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't share your data with anyone except the services that run Kova for us. Bank statements you import are read on your phone, and only the rows you confirm are saved. You can export or delete everything from Settings at any time.

Who we are

Kova is an AI money companion made by Ambora Labs ("Kova", "we", "us"). If you have a question about this policy or your data, email support.amboralabs@gmail.com.

We don't collect your location, contacts, photos, advertising ID or browsing history, and we don't use analytics or ad trackers.

Statements you import

When you import a bank or wallet statement (CSV), Kova reads the file on your phone. The file itself is not uploaded or stored. Two things leave your phone:

  • Merchant names Kova couldn't sort by itself are sent to our server and to Google's Gemini AI to pick a category. Amounts and dates aren't sent with them.
  • If your phone can't read a file's layout, a small file (under about 90 KB) can be sent to our server and Gemini to be read. It's processed and then discarded, not stored.

Only the rows you review and confirm are saved to your account.

Voice notes

Voice notes are turned into text by your device's or browser's own speech service (in Chrome, for example, that's Google's speech service). Kova receives only the text, never the recording.

On your device

To open quickly, Kova keeps a copy of your entries, letters and settings on the device you use. Signing out or deleting your account removes that copy from the device. Your chosen theme is also remembered on the device.

How we use it

  • To run Kova: show your spending, goals and subscriptions, answer your questions, and write your letters.
  • To personalise Kova's replies to your situation and the tone you chose.
  • To run your account, apply plan limits, confirm purchases and send reminders you allowed.
  • To keep Kova secure and working, for example rate limits and error logs.

We don't sell your data, use it for advertising, or use it to make decisions about credit or lending. Kova explains money; it doesn't give personal investment advice.

AI

Kova's replies, letters, lessons and some categorising are written by Google's Gemini models through Google Cloud Vertex AI. To answer you, the relevant parts of your data (for example your message, recent entries and goals) are sent to Gemini. Google processes it as our service provider under Google Cloud's terms, which don't allow Google to use it to train its models. AI replies can be wrong. To report one, press and hold the reply (or use its ⋯ menu on a computer) and choose Report reply. It's sent to us for review.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers that run Kova for us, and only for that purpose:

  • Google Firebase and Google Cloud: sign-in, database, hosting, notifications and Gemini AI.
  • Google Play: app distribution and Premium payments.
  • Channel3: when you ask "Can I afford…" about a specific product without giving a price, only the product name is sent to Channel3 to look up a current price. If you tap "I'll wait", Kova keeps that product (name, price and store) for up to 30 days and checks its price with Channel3 from time to time, so it can tell you about a drop. Nothing about your money or your account is sent to Channel3.
  • RevenueCat: confirming Premium purchases.

We'll also disclose data if the law requires it. If Kova is ever sold or merged, we'll tell you before your data moves to a new owner.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest by Google Cloud. Database rules let only you read your own data, and fields like your plan can be changed only by our server. Our AI keys and other secrets are kept on the server, never in the app.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and money data: until you delete your account. Deleting an expense or income entry removes it straight away.
  • Reported replies: if you report a Kova reply, we keep that reply so we can review it, until you delete your account.
  • Imported statement files: never stored.
  • Server logs (errors and request records, without your chat text or statement contents): up to 30 days.
  • Purchase records: Google Play keeps its own order records under Google's policies.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Export: Settings → Data & Privacy → Export my data gives you a copy in JSON.
  • Delete in the app: Settings → Data & Privacy → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, money entries, goals, chats, letters, settings, device tokens, reported replies and purchase record from our systems. For your safety, it needs a sign-in from the last 10 minutes. Deleted data may stay in Google Cloud's systems for a short time before it's permanently removed.
  • Delete without the app: see how to request deletion.
  • Premium: deleting your account doesn't cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it in Google Play first, under Payments & subscriptions.
  • Notifications: you can turn them off in your phone's settings at any time.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to access, correct or object to the use of your data. Email us and we'll help within 30 days.

Children

Kova is for adults aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you think a child has created an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For important changes, we'll let you know in the app.

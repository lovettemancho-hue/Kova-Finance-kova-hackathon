Delete your Kova account

You can delete your Kova account and all its data at any time, in the app or by email. Kova is made by Ambora Labs.

In the app

  1. Open Kova and go to Settings.
  2. Under Data & Privacy, tap Delete account, then Delete.
  3. If Kova asks you to sign in again, sign out, sign back in, and delete again. For your safety, deleting needs a recent sign-in.

Deletion happens straight away.

Without the app

If you've uninstalled Kova or can't sign in, email us from the address you use for Kova and we'll delete your account within 30 days.

Email a deletion request

support.amboralabs@gmail.com. We'll confirm by email once it's done. We may ask you to confirm the request from your account's email address so no one else can delete your account.

Before you delete: Premium

Deleting your account doesn't cancel a Premium subscription. Cancel it first in the Google Play Store app: tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Kova → Cancel.

What's deleted

  • Your account and sign-in (email, name, Google sign-in link)
  • Your setup answers, tone, country and currency
  • All expenses and income you logged or imported, goals and contributions, subscription notes
  • Your chats with Kova, goal coaching, monthly letters and lessons
  • Notification device tokens, usage counts, replies you reported, and your purchase record with RevenueCat

What's kept, and for how long

  • Server logs (errors and request records, without your chat text or statement contents) are kept for up to 30 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Deleted data may stay in Google Cloud's systems for a short time before it's permanently removed.
  • Google Play keeps its own records of any purchases under Google's policies.

Want a copy first? In Settings → Data & Privacy, tap Export my data. More in our Privacy Policy.
